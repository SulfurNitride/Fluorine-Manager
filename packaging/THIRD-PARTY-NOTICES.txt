Fluorine Manager third-party notices
=====================================

This directory accompanies the exact binary bundle. It contains:

- license texts from source components compiled or copied into the bundle;
- target-reachable Cargo package license files;
- Python runtime and shipped Python-distribution license metadata;
- SPDX software-bill-of-materials documents for the shipped Qt modules; and
- distribution copyright files for shared libraries copied from the builder.

The files describe their respective components and retain their upstream
copyright notices. Their inclusion does not change Fluorine Manager's own
GPL-3.0-or-later license, provided as ../LICENSE.txt.
